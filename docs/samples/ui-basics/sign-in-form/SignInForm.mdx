# 02. Sign-in form

A form that refuses you first. Submit it empty, then with `nope@` in the email
field, then with the wrong password — the errors land in three different places
and none of them needed layout work.

<Story of={SignInFormStories.Default} />

The password this demo portal accepts is **`Docs2026!`**.

## Where the pieces do the work

- **`FormWrapper`** is the card: padding, border and background that match every
  other sign-in screen in the product.
- **`FieldContainer`** owns the label, the required asterisk and the error line
  under the field. Validation becomes two pieces of state — `hasError` and
  `errorMessage` — instead of a layout exercise.
- **`EmailInput`** parses the address itself and reports through
  `onValidateInput`. The form decides _when_ to listen: on submit, never while
  the user is still mid-address, which is the difference between helpful and
  nagging.
- **`PasswordInput`** with `simpleView` is the sign-in variant — one field with
  the reveal eye. Drop `simpleView` and it grows the strength tooltip and the
  generator, which is what a _set a new password_ screen wants.

Two habits worth copying from this file: clearing the error in `onChange`, so a
correction is rewarded immediately, and `noValidate` on the `<form>`, so the
browser's own bubbles stay out of the way of the component's error line.
